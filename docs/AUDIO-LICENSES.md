# Audio and font licenses

Everything here was made for Wave Catch. No marketplace packs, no sampled recordings, and no third-party game audio.

## Music and sound effects

Generated by `Scripts/synthesize_audio.py` (Python, numpy, then ffmpeg AAC). Each note has a short attack and release so loops and one-shots do not click. The files live in `WaveCatchApp/WaveCatch/Audio/`.

The loop is a pentatonic lead, a soft bass, and light drums. Playback keeps that loop under the sound effects: the base stem plays at 0.18 and the Big Wave stem at 0.14, while the catch chime plays at 0.85 and the wood-block tick at 0.20. Music and sound effects each have their own toggle. Both default to on and are stored in UserDefaults.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Rubik Regular | WaveCatchApp/WaveCatch/Fonts/Rubik-Regular.ttf | [The Rubik Project](https://github.com/googlefonts/rubik), weight 400 instance of the variable font | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

The app registers both files in `UIAppFonts`. Numbers and callouts use Bungee. Body text uses Rubik.
